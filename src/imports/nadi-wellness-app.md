Design a premium mobile wellness app experience called NADI.

NADI is a cognitive energy awareness system that combines a wristband and a mobile app. The purpose of the product is to help users sense their cognitive energy levels, capture thoughts when overwhelmed, and revisit them later when they have the capacity to act.

The product should feel calm, warm, minimal, trustworthy, and understandable for first-time users without any explanation.

The interface should feel like a real premium wellness product, not futuristic or sci-fi.

Use iPhone 14 Pro mobile screen size for all screens.
