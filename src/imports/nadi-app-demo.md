Create a mobile app demo for a speculative wellness product called NADI.

The app helps users sense, track, and respond to invisible cognitive and energetic states before burnout. The concept is grounded in an Indian-inspired framework with three channels, Ida, Pingala, and Sushumna, three energy dimensions, Prana, Tejas, and Ojas, and five cognitive states, Kshipta, Mudha, Vikshipta, Ekagra, and Niruddha.

This app should feel beginner-friendly, premium, calm, visually warm, and instantly understandable for first-time users without any explanation or demo. The interface should be clean, minimal, intuitive, and grounded in plausible real-world use. Avoid sci-fi UI, overly technical dashboards, and clutter. This should look like a believable wellness and cognitive support app, not a medical device app.

Design each screen so the app feels interactive and sensible. The user should be able to understand what to do next on every screen.

Detailed screen requirements:

Welcome screen
Show NADI as the hero. Include one short line explaining the purpose, such as helping users feel their energy before it runs out. Include clear Create Account and I already have an account buttons.

Sign in or create account
Show email and password fields, Continue button, and Sign in with Apple and Google. Keep it simple and elegant.

Permissions setup
Explain clearly why heart rate, motion, calendar, and voice capture permissions are needed. Voice must be clearly described as only captured when the user actively presses the button. Include toggles or permission cards.

Connect calendar
Allow the user to connect Apple Calendar or Google Calendar. Explain that NADI uses time windows and schedule context to surface captured thoughts when energy is high enough to act.

How NADI works
Teach the product simply and visually.
Framework 1: Ida, Pingala, Sushumna
Framework 2: Prana, Tejas, Ojas
Framework 3: five cognitive states, Kshipta, Mudha, Vikshipta, Ekagra, Niruddha
This screen should feel educational but not dense.

Home dashboard
This is the most important screen.
It should show:
- a premium Nadi visualization as the main hero element
- current cognitive state
- Prana, Tejas, and Ojas cards
- one useful insight
- bottom navigation to Home, Speak, Thoughts, Tasks, and Restore
The Nadi visualization should feel alive, balanced, organic, and central to the experience.

Voice capture recording
Minimal interface. Show one large recording button and soft ripple or listening animation. The copy should reassure the user that they can speak tasks, worries, ideas, or questions and NADI will hold them until the right time.

Voice capture captured
Show a successful captured state using sage green. Include a short message that NADI will surface the thought when the user has the right energy and time. Keep this screen emotionally calm and rewarding.

Captured thoughts inbox
Show categorized captured items such as ideas, tasks, worries, and questions. Include filters and readiness states like waiting for high capacity, fits in a 10 minute gap, or parked.

Resurfaced thought
This is a key moment. Show that the user’s current energy is high enough and that a previously captured thought is now ready to revisit. Include a highlighted resurfaced card with context and actions like Develop now and Park for later.

Tasks and calendar fit
Show today’s schedule with open windows and NADI suggesting the best next task based on calendar gaps and current energy. Make this feel plausible and practical.

Weekly patterns
Show a beautiful, digestible analytics screen with weekly energy patterns, golden hours, Ojas low points, top drains, and distribution of the five cognitive states. Make it elegant, not technical overload.

Restore mode
Show personalized restore options when Ojas is low. Include cards such as a 3 minute reset, Yoga Nidra, or a short walk, and a clear primary call to action.

Interaction requirements
Make the app feel like a real tappable demo. Include:
- smooth transitions between screens
- subtle breathing or pulsing animation for the Nadi visualization
- ripple animation for voice capture
- a meaningful confirmation transition for captured voice input
- a highlighted resurfaced thought moment
- bottom navigation interactions
- scrollable screens where needed

Important design goals
- Make the app easy for a first-time user to understand immediately
- Keep all screens visually consistent
- Make the Home dashboard the strongest screen
- Make the app feel premium, calm, and culturally grounded
- Avoid clutter, generic health dashboard style, neon effects, and excessive text
- Make the design look polished enough for a hackathon showcase and a demo video
