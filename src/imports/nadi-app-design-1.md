Design a premium mobile wellness app experience called NADI.

NADI is a wristband + mobile app system designed for young adults. The system gives users a new sense: the ability to feel their cognitive energy and resilience before burnout takes hold.

The wristband senses physiological signals and communicates energy shifts through subtle temperature changes. The mobile app helps users understand these signals, capture thoughts when overwhelmed, and revisit them later when they have the capacity to act.

The design should feel calm, warm, minimal, premium, and emotionally trustworthy. The product must look believable and grounded in real life.

Use iPhone 14 Pro screen size for all screens.
